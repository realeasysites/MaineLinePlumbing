Our Story

Local, licensed, and hands-on.

Maine-Line Plumbing LLC was founded in Buxton, Maine by a Master Plumber with more than 8 years in the trade. After years of running other people's jobs, the goal was simple: build a plumbing company that treats every homeowner's house like it matters — because it does.

As a veteran-owned business, Maine-Line brings the same discipline and attention to detail from the field to every water heater swap, remodel, and repair call. No subcontracted crews, no upselling — just straightforward plumbing work done to code and built to last.

  • Maine trade license, active through 2027
  • 8+ years of hands-on plumbing experience
  • Veteran owned & operated
  • Serving Buxton & all of York County
